## Spread 1 — Mirai's bedroom, early morning

Outside, the first birds were waking up.
Inside, Mirai was still asleep,
curled up small and warm.

Ever since the day Mirai was born,
Mama had called her by one special word.

Mama bent down and whispered, warm against her ear:

Mama: Wake up, Koshakanym.

It was Mama's word.
Just for Mirai.

## Spread 2 — the bathroom, morning

Warm water ran from the tap. Splish, splash.
Mirai caught it in her hooves,
and it wriggled right through.

Mirai: It tickles!

Mama flicked one tiny drop onto her cheek.

Mirai: Mama! Now I'm all drippy!
Mama: A drippy Koshakan.
Mama: And a very clean one.

## Spread 3 — the dandelion meadow, afternoon

The meadow smelled of grass and warm earth.
Mirai ran, and ran, and jumped,
right into the dandelions.

Puff! Puff! Puff!

Mirai: Mama, look! I'm making snow!
Mama: Summer snow! Run, Koshakanym, run!

It was Mama's word.
Just for Mirai.

## Spread 4 — the dandelion meadow, down in the grass

Faster, faster… whoops!
Down went Mirai, bump, into the grass.
It was cool and tickly on her back.

Mama sat down beside her.

Mama: Up you get, Koshakanym.

But Mirai didn't get up.
She laughed and laughed until her tummy wobbled.
So Mama laughed too.

Mama: Oh, what a giggly Koshakan you are!

## Spread 5 — Mama's lap, in the meadow

Mirai climbed into Mama's lap.
It was soft and warm, like a nest in the grass.

High above them, a slow white cloud drifted by.

Mama: Rest with me, Koshakanym.
Mirai: Just for a little bit.

Mirai was getting bigger every day.
But Mama still called her the very same thing.

It was Mama's word.
Just for Mirai.

## Spread 6 — Mama's lap, in the meadow

Mirai watched the cloud for a long, long time.
A dandelion seed floated past her nose.

She had never wondered before.
But today, she did.

Mirai: Mama?
Mama: Mm?
Mirai: You always call me Koshakan.
Mirai: What does Koshakan mean?

## Spread 7 — Mama's lap, the meadow in golden light

Mama held her a little closer.

Mama: Koshakan means a little lamb.
Mama: And Koshakanym means *my* little lamb.

Mirai: A lamb?

Mama: A lamb is small and tender.
Its wool is soft as dandelion fluff,
and warm as milk at bedtime.
A lamb is gentle. And kind.
All the way through.

## Spread 8 — Mama's lap, under the evening sky

Mama: When you sleep, curled up tight,
I see a little lamb.
When you run through the grass,
I see a little lamb.
And when you laugh…
I hear one.

Mama: A Koshakan is someone loved very, very much.
Someone you always, always take care of.

## Spread 9 — Mama's lap, the hushed meadow

Mirai didn't say anything.
Mama didn't say anything either.
Even the dandelions held still.

Mirai laid her ear on Mama's chest
and listened.

Thump-thump.
Thump-thump.
Slow and soft and close.

It was Mama's heart.
Just for Mirai.

## Spread 10 — Mama's lap, close together in the warm light

Somewhere in the grass, the crickets began to sing.

Mirai lifted her head.
She looked at her own small hooves.
Then up at Mama.
She spoke so softly that only Mama could hear.

Mirai: Mama… so I'm like a little lamb?

Mama: Yes.
Mama: My little, most-loved Koshakan.

## Spread 11 — the dandelion meadow at sunset

Mirai hugged Mama tight.
Tighter.
As tight as she possibly could.

Mirai: Then I'll always be your Koshakan.
Mirai: Always and always.
Mama: Always, Koshakanym.

The sun was warm on their backs.

From that day on, Koshakan was never just a word.
It felt like a hug.

It was Mama's word.
And now it was Mirai's, too.
